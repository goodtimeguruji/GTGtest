Vidyarambham Muhurat

Vidyarambham marks the formal beginning of a child's education, and is closely related to Aksharaarambham in several regional traditions.

Find Your Vidyarambham Muhurat

Why This Muhurat Matters

Beginning schooling or a new phase of education is timed carefully in many households, as it is seen as setting the tone for a child's learning years.

What We Consider

Every Vidyarambham Muhurat is worked out from a combination of traditional Panchang elements together with your submitted birth and location details:

Nakshatra

Your birth star, used as the base reference for compatibility checks.

Tithi

The lunar day, checked across your selected date range.

Yoga

The Nakshatra-based Yoga combination for each candidate date.

Karana

Half-Tithi divisions considered alongside the day's Panchang.

Chandrabalam

Moon-strength for your Rasi on each candidate date.

Tarabalam

Star-strength relative to your Nakshatra.

Rahu Kaal

The daily inauspicious window, avoided where possible.

Yamaganda

Another traditionally avoided time segment each day.

Location & Timings

Your selected location, used to compute local sunrise-based timings.

Who Is This For?

  • Parents planning their child's start of formal education
  • Families following regional Vidyarambham customs
  • Anyone wanting a considered date before school admission or the first day of classes
