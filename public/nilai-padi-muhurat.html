Nilai Padi Muhurat

Nilai Padi refers to the ceremony of installing the main entrance threshold/doorframe of a home, a step considered significant in traditional home-building customs.

Find Your Nilai Padi Muhurat

Why This Muhurat Matters

The main entrance is regarded as an important part of a home's structure, and many families like to time its installation with care.

What We Consider

Every Nilai Padi Muhurat is worked out from a combination of traditional Panchang elements together with your submitted birth and location details:

Nakshatra

Your birth star, used as the base reference for compatibility checks.

Tithi

The lunar day, checked across your selected date range.

Yoga

The Nakshatra-based Yoga combination for each candidate date.

Karana

Half-Tithi divisions considered alongside the day's Panchang.

Chandrabalam

Moon-strength for your Rasi on each candidate date.

Tarabalam

Star-strength relative to your Nakshatra.

Rahu Kaal

The daily inauspicious window, avoided where possible.

Yamaganda

Another traditionally avoided time segment each day.

Location & Timings

Your selected location, used to compute local sunrise-based timings.

Who Is This For?

  • Homeowners at the doorframe/threshold installation stage of construction
  • Builders coordinating this milestone with the client's preferences
  • Families following traditional home-construction customs
